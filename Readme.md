# 🌍 Solmate  
**Your smart travel companion — from planning to exploring.**

Solmate is a group-first travel planning and assistance platform that helps travelers reduce chaos, avoid app-hopping, and plan trips together in one shared space.

Instead of using WhatsApp, Google Docs, Splitwise, and multiple travel apps, Solmate brings everything into one simple, collaborative experience.

---

## 🚀 What Problem Does Solmate Solve?

Planning a trip today is fragmented:

- Booking apps help with reservations  
- Review apps help with suggestions  
- Maps help with navigation  
- Chat apps handle coordination  
- Expense apps track money  

But **no single platform manages the entire travel planning workflow**, especially for groups.

**Solmate fixes this.**

---

## 💡 What Is Solmate?

Solmate is a **Traveler Operating System** that supports users:

- **Before the trip** → planning & coordination  
- **During the trip** → assistance & tracking  
- **After the trip** → memories & insights *(future)*  

The MVP focuses on **group travel planning**, with future expansion into a **travel buddy AI** and **traveler community**.

---

## 🎯 MVP Focus

The first version of Solmate focuses on **one core goal**:

> Help a group go from “Let’s plan a trip” → “We have a clear plan and budget” without chaos.

---

## ✨ Core MVP Features

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Group Trip Spaces
- Create a trip and invite members
- Everything related to the trip lives in one shared space

### 📅 Date & Budget Voting
- Vote on preferred travel dates and budgets
- Automatically identify the best option

### 🗺 Shared Itinerary Builder
- Day-wise trip planning
- Assign responsibilities to members

### 💰 Expense & Budget Tracker
- Track who paid for what
- Automatic expense splitting
- Clear balance overview

### 🔔 Smart Reminders
- Payment reminders
- Booking deadlines
- Trip start alerts

### 💬 Trip Chat (Lightweight)
- Contextual chat inside each trip
- Reduces dependency on external messaging apps
